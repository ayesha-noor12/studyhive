# Scope: StudyHive

## One-line pitch
Upload a PDF, get it explained section by section, take a quiz when ready, and get your mistakes re-explained.

## Who it's for
University students who have lecture notes or chapters to study and want quick, grounded explanations and self-testing.

## In scope (must work end to end)
1. Upload a text-based PDF
2. Explain the content section by section
3. "I'm ready" button starts a quiz (5 MCQs grounded in the PDF)
4. Score the quiz
5. Re-explain each wrong answer, citing the section of the PDF

## Out of scope (not in this draft)
- OCR / scanned PDFs
- Accounts, login, saved history
- Learning-style profiles
- Web search / resource finder
- Roadmaps, spaced repetition, PDF export
- Multiple interfaces (web only)

## Success = demo
A 2-minute screen recording: upload PDF, read explanation, take quiz, miss a question, see it re-explained.

## Constraints
- Streamlit UI, hosted LLM API (Groq or OpenAI)
- New repo from an empty folder; planning docs committed first
